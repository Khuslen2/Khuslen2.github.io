# Irodov 1.1 — Motorboat and Raft

[← Back to Irodov problems](/)

## Test

Let the velocity of the river flow be \(v_f\).

The distance travelled by the raft is

$$
l = v_f T.
$$

Therefore,

$$
v_f = \frac{l}{T}.
$$

For this problem,

$$
\boxed{v_f = 0.83\ \mathrm{m/s}}.
$$
