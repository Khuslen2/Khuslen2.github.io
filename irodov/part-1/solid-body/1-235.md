# Irodov Problem 1.235

## Problem

A force

$$
\mathbf{F}=A\mathbf{i}+B\mathbf{j}
$$

is applied to a point whose radius vector relative to the origin of coordinates \(O\) is

$$
\mathbf{r}=a\mathbf{i}+b\mathbf{j},
$$

where \(a,b,A,B\) are constants, and \(\mathbf{i},\mathbf{j}\) are the unit vectors of the \(x\)- and \(y\)-axes.

Find the **moment \(N\)** and the **arm \(l\)** of the force \(\mathbf{F}\) relative to the point \(O\).

---

## Solution

The moment of a force about the origin is given by the vector product

$$
\mathbf{N}=\mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{F}.
$$

Substituting

$$
\mathbf{r}=a\mathbf{i}+b\mathbf{j}
$$

and

$$
\mathbf{F}=A\mathbf{i}+B\mathbf{j},
$$

we obtain

$$
\mathbf{N}
=
(a\mathbf{i}+b\mathbf{j})
\times
(A\mathbf{i}+B\mathbf{j}).
$$

Expanding the cross product,

$$
\mathbf{N}
=
aB(\mathbf{i}\times\mathbf{j})
+
bA(\mathbf{j}\times\mathbf{i}).
$$

Since

$$
\mathbf{i}\times\mathbf{j}=\mathbf{k},
\qquad
\mathbf{j}\times\mathbf{i}=-\mathbf{k},
$$

we get

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{N}=(aB-bA)\mathbf{k}}.
$$

Therefore, the magnitude of the moment is

$$
N=|aB-bA|.
$$

The magnitude of the force is

$$
F=\sqrt{A^2+B^2}.
$$

Since the magnitude of the moment is related to the arm \(l\) by

$$
N=Fl,
$$

the arm of the force is

$$
l=\frac{N}{F}.
$$

Hence,

$$
\boxed{
l=\frac{|aB-bA|}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2}}
}.
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{N}=(aB-bA)\mathbf{k}}
$$

$$
\boxed{
l=\frac{|aB-bA|}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2}}
}
$$
