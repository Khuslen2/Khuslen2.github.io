# Irodov 1.234 — Translational Motion of a Rod

[← Back to Dynamics of a Solid Body](/irodov/part-1/solid-body.html)

## Problem

A thin uniform rod of mass \(m=1.0\,\mathrm{kg}\) moves translationally with acceleration

$$
w=2.0\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
$$

under the action of two antiparallel forces \(F_1\) and \(F_2\).

The distance between their points of application is

$$
a=20\,\mathrm{cm},
$$

and

$$
F_2=5.0\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

Find the length of the rod.

## My Solution

Let \(O\) be the center of mass of the uniform rod.

Since the rod is uniform,

$$
OA=OB=x.
$$

Therefore, the length of the rod is

$$
AB=2x.
$$

### 1. Translational motion

The rod moves translationally with acceleration \(w\).

Taking the direction of \(F_2\) as positive, Newton's second law gives

$$
F_2-F_1=mw.
$$

Therefore,

$$
F_1=F_2-mw.
$$

Substituting the given values,

$$
F_1
=5.0-(1.0)(2.0)
=3.0\,\mathrm{N}.
$$

So,

$$
\boxed{F_1=3.0\,\mathrm{N}}.
$$

### 2. Rotational condition

The important point is that the rod moves **translationally**.

Therefore, it does not acquire angular acceleration. The net torque about its center of mass \(O\) must be zero:

$$
\sum \tau_O=0.
$$

The distance from \(O\) to the point where \(F_1\) acts is \(x\).

Since the point of application of \(F_2\) is a distance \(a\) below \(A\), its distance from \(O\) is

$$
x-a.
$$

Therefore,

$$
F_2(x-a)-F_1x=0.
$$

Rearranging,

$$
F_2x-F_2a-F_1x=0,
$$

so

$$
(F_2-F_1)x=F_2a.
$$

Hence,

$$
x=\frac{F_2a}{F_2-F_1}.
$$

Substituting

$$
F_2=5.0\,\mathrm{N},\qquad
F_1=3.0\,\mathrm{N},\qquad
a=0.20\,\mathrm{m},
$$

we obtain

$$
x
=
\frac{(5.0)(0.20)}{5.0-3.0}
=
0.50\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
AB=2x=1.0\,\mathrm{m}.
$$

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{AB=1.0\,\mathrm{m}}
$$

---

## What I Learned

The word **translationally** is the key to this problem.

The center of mass can accelerate,

$$
\sum F = mw,
$$

while the rod simultaneously has no angular acceleration. Therefore,

$$
\sum \tau_O=0.
$$

Using both the translational and rotational conditions determines the length of the rod.
