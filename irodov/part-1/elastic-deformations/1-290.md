Heating tends to increase the length of the cylinder,

$$
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{thermal}}
=
+\alpha\Delta T,
$$

while the pressure applied to its ends produces compression,

$$
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{compression}}
=
-\frac{p}{E}.
$$

Because the cylinder must keep the same length,

$$
\boxed{
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{total}}=0
}
$$

and therefore

$$
\boxed{
\alpha\Delta T-\frac{p}{E}=0
}.
$$
